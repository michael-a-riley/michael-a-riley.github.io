What we can help with

Walk up to the kiosk or send a message. Tours and transfers are run by licensed local partners; we book them for you at the posted price.

  • Old City walks

    Two to three hours with a licensed guide, in Arabic, English or French.

  • Day trips

    Maaloula, Bosra, Palmyra, Krak des Chevaliers. A vetted driver and a guide if you like.

  • Hands-on experiences

    Cooking in a Damascene home, a calligraphy hour, a brocade workshop, a hammam.

  • Countryside and farm stays

    Sleep on a farm, pick pistachios in Morek, see the norias of Hama and the Houran.

  • Transfers and taxis

    Airport, Lebanon border, Amman, or a driver by the hour. Fixed prices.

  • Where to stay

    Partner guesthouses and hostels in Bab Touma and Qaymariyya.

  • Free help

    Directions, SIM, exchange, phone charging, a paper map.
