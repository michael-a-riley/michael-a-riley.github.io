Free help

This is what the kiosk exists for. Nothing here costs anything, apart from phone credit at face value.

  • Directions and a paper map

    A map of the Old City in Arabic and English with a you-are-here mark. We draw your route on it.

  • Getting a SIM

    A SIM must be registered with your passport at an authorised centre. We walk you to the nearest one (5 minutes) and help with the paperwork. After that, top up with us.

  • Exchange and cash

    We don't change money. We tell you today's rate and point you to a licensed exchange office nearby. The new pound has two fewer zeros; mind the notes.

  • Phone charging

    Cables for every phone and a power bank. Free with any coffee.

  • Toilets and pharmacies

    We know the nearest clean toilet and the nearest open pharmacy.

  • Prayer and service times

    Umayyad Mosque visiting hours and Bab Touma church service times, on the board every day.

  • Emergency numbers

    Ambulance 110, police 112, fire 113. For a complaint about a tourism service, the Ministry of Tourism's Labaeik platform.
