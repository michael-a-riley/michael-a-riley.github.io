Damascus · TVET · livelihoods · farm design

I work on technical training, rural livelihoods, and farm design.

Mostly in Syria and Lebanon. The common thread is skills and systems that don't get recorded — what displaced workers actually know, how land actually holds water — and turning that into something usable.
