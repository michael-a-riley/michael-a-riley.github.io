Walks in the Old City

Every walk is led by a guide licensed by the Ministry of Tourism and starts at the kiosk. Book today for tomorrow, or drop by in the morning and see what's free.

  • The classic: Umayyad Mosque and Hamidiyeh

    3 hours · 9:00, 15:00

    First day in Damascus? This is the one. From Bab Touma along Straight Street to Souq al-Hamidiyeh, the Umayyad Mosque, Azm Palace and the Nawfara café.

    From $20 per person

  • The Christian quarter and Straight Street

    2 hours · 10:00, 16:00

    The Mariamite Cathedral, the underground Chapel of Ananias, Bab Kisan and the St Paul story, and the courtyard houses of Bab Touma and Jabri House.

    From $15 per person

  • Souq food walk

    3 hours · 11:00

    Fatteh for breakfast, shawarma and falafel, Bakdash ice cream, Ghraoui sweets and coffee on the way. Six stops; you finish full.

    From $25 per person

  • Evening walk to Bab Sharqi

    2 hours · 19:00

    Damascus after dark is another city. A slow walk from Bab Touma to Bab Sharqi through lit lanes and old houses turned restaurants, ending with a dinner recommendation.

    From $15 per person

  • Qasioun at sunset

    3 hours · Two hours before sunset

    A car from the kiosk up Mount Qasioun, the whole city below you, and tea at a hillside café as the lights come on. Back to Bab Touma after dark.

    From $30 per person

  • Damascene houses and a hammam

    4 hours · 14:00

    Inside three courtyard houses (Maktab Anbar, Beit Nizam and a private home that opens its door for us), then Hammam Nur al-Din or Hammam al-Bakri depending on the hour.

    From $35 per person
