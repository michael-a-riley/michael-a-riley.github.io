Day trips

A private car with a vetted driver from the kiosk, back in Damascus the same day. A guide is optional and priced separately. Prices are per car for up to 3 people; a 7-seater is available for groups.

  • Maaloula and Saydnaya

    6 hours · Leaves 9:00

    The last village where Aramaic is still spoken, the Mar Taqla convent and the gorge through the rock, then the Convent of Our Lady of Saydnaya on its hill. The closest trip and the best half day.

    From $70 per car

  • Bosra

    8 hours · Leaves 8:00

    The best-preserved Roman theatre in the region, built in black basalt inside a citadel. Two hours south through the Hauran plain, lunch there.

    From $110 per car

  • Palmyra

    A long day, 12 hours · Leaves 6:00

    Three hours into the desert to Zenobia's city: the colonnade, the Temple of Bel, the theatre and the hilltop castle. We check the road situation before every booking.

    From $220 per car

  • Krak des Chevaliers

    10 hours · Leaves 7:00

    The greatest Crusader castle anywhere, on a hill above the Valley of the Christians. Two and a half hours north, with Homs and the St George monastery as optional stops.

    From $180 per car

  • Zabadani and Bloudan

    6 hours · Leaves 10:00

    Where Damascenes escape the heat. An hour's drive to the Anti-Lebanon mountains: cool air, cherries and apricots in summer, lunch on a terrace.

    From $60 per car

  • Farm lunch in the Ghouta

    5 hours · Leaves 11:00, Fridays and Saturdays

    Half an hour out of the city to an orchard in the Ghouta: pick whatever is in season and eat a home-cooked lunch under the trees with the family who farm it.

    From $45 per person

Prices are indicative and confirmed when you book. You pay the partner directly.
