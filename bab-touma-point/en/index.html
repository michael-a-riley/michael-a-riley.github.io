Before you explore

The questions visitors ask us most. Ask at the kiosk for anything more recent.

  • Bring cash Most places take cash only. US dollars and euros are widely accepted; change them at a licensed exchange office, and we can point you to the nearest one. The Syrian pound lost two zeros in 2026, so check which notes you are given.
  • Dress for mosques and churches Cover shoulders and knees. Women cover their hair in mosques; cloaks are usually available at the Umayyad Mosque entrance.
  • Getting around The Old City is best on foot. For anything further, agree the taxi fare before you get in, or ask us for a driver at a fixed price.
  • Staying connected A local SIM is the easiest option. Bring your passport to register it.

Bab Touma Point is an independent kiosk, not a government office. Tours, trips and transfers are provided by licensed partners.
