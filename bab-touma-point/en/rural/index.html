Countryside and farm stays

The Old City is beautiful, but the real Syria is outside the walls. With families and farmers we know by name in Hama, the Houran, the Qalamoun and the Ghouta. Most of these are seasonal, so check the season before you book.

  • Pistachio harvest in Morek

    2 or 3 nights · August and September

    Morek, in the Hama countryside, is pistachio country. You sleep with a farming family, wake early and pick with them, watch the hulling and drying, and eat what they cook. Not a show: this is their work and you are their guest.

    From $55 per person a night

  • A night on a Ghouta farm

    1 night or more · All year

    Half an hour from Bab Touma and another world. A room in the farmhouse, dinner and breakfast from the land, and a morning with the sheep and the orchard. The easiest countryside experience and the closest.

    From $40 per person a night

  • Damask rose harvest in al-Mrah

    A long day · May only

    The Damask rose used in the world's perfumes grows in the village of al-Mrah in the Qalamoun. Pick with the villagers before sunrise, watch the distilling, and take home a bottle of rose water. The rose festival is at the end of May.

    From $60 per person

  • Hama: the norias and Apamea

    A long day, or overnight · All year

    Hama's wooden waterwheels on the Orontes, halawet el-jibn in the souq, then the Roman colonnade of Apamea running two kilometres across the plain. Add a night with a family in the Hama countryside and it becomes a different trip.

    From $190 per car

  • Olive picking in the Houran

    A full day · October and November

    The black basalt plain south of Damascus. Pick olives with a family, go to the press, and eat lunch under the trees. Add Bosra in the afternoon and you've seen the whole south.

    From $45 per person

  • Cheese and mouneh in the Qalamoun

    Half a day · All year

    A morning with a sheep-farming family in the Qalamoun mountains: milking, village cheese, labneh, then the season's pantry work: makdous, zaatar, laurel soap. You take something home.

    From $30 per person

  • Inclusive group trips

    3 to 10 days · By arrangement

    For groups of 6 to 16 coming from abroad: vehicles, accommodation split between Damascus and the countryside, guides, and every experience above, at one price. We work with Syrian partners overseas who organise the group at their end while we handle this end.

    Ask for a price
